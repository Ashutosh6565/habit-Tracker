## Full description (max 4000)

```
Habbiters is a free habit tracker that makes showing up feel good. Pick a habit, check it off, watch your streak grow — and keep your whole day, not just your habits, in one calm place.

No paywall. No trial. Every habit, every template, every screen is free.

MOMENTUM — THE SCORE THAT SEES YOUR WHOLE DAY
Most trackers reduce your day to a single checkbox. Momentum blends your habits, tasks and focused hours into one honest score from 0 to 100. It shows your last seven days, tells you how you moved since yesterday, and always names the next best move — the one action that lifts your score right now. One missed habit never defines your day.

START FROM A PROVEN ROUTINE
105 free templates for fitness, study, focus, sleep, money and daily discipline. Open one, choose the habits you want, and they land in your tracker in one tap. Mix habits from several routines if that is what your life looks like.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Today dashboard — greet the day, see the Momentum score, your week at a glance, and tap a habit to check it in
• One-tap templates — 105 routines, free, ready to become your habits
• Streaks that survive real life — miss a day, keep the habit, start again
• XP, levels and achievements for every completion
• Weekly time tracker — log focused hours in a simple 7-day grid
• Analytics that tell the truth — consistency, best days, time invested and trends
• Tasks with a time-ordered timeline, grouped by category
• Calendar and notes — a month view, an activity heatmap and a running log of thoughts
• Gentle reminders so habits find you, not the other way around

BUILT FOR REAL LIFE
Most habit apps ask for a perfect month. Habbiters asks for one small rep today. The mobile app is designed for the way people actually behave: quick taps, clear progress, and no guilt when a busy week happens.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Your habits belong to you. No ads, no selling your data, no third-party trackers. You can delete your account and all of your data at any time from Profile → Delete account, or from habbiters.app/delete-account.

FREE, FOR REAL
Habit building is hard enough without your tools working against your wallet. Habbiters is free to use in full — the tracker, all 105 templates, the planner, time tracking, analytics and gamification. There is nothing to unlock.

Habbiters is made for people who are tired of starting over.
```

## What to avoid in the listing

- Do not promise medical, financial, or mental-health outcomes.
- Do not use "best", "#1", or rankings you cannot prove.
- Do not claim ratings or download numbers you cannot prove.
- Do not mention Razorpay, UPI, or outside payment methods.
- Do not use the Google Play logo or "Download on Google Play" badges inside the listing text.
